Minecraft 1.16 Nether Update: what's new

Minecraft 1.16 (Nether Update) is a major Java Edition update released on June 23, 2020. On Bedrock Edition the same update is version 1.16.0, released June 23, 2020. Its biggest additions: New Nether biomes: Crimson Forest, Warped Forest, Soul Sand Valley, Basalt Deltas; Piglins (they barter for gold), Hoglins, Striders and Zoglins; Netherite gear, made from ancient debris.

What's new in 1.16

Bold lines are the headline changes.

Mobs

  • Piglins (they barter for gold), Hoglins, Striders and Zoglins

Blocks

  • Respawn anchor, lodestone, blackstone, crimson and warped wood, soul fire, chains, target block

Items

  • Netherite gear, made from ancient debris

World & biomes

  • New Nether biomes: Crimson Forest, Warped Forest, Soul Sand Valley, Basalt Deltas
  • Bastion remnants and ruined portals

Gameplay

  • Soul Speed enchantment; music disc "Pigstep"

Heads-up

  • The Nether only gets the new biomes in areas you haven't explored yet
