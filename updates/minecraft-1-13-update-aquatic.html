Minecraft 1.13 Update Aquatic: what's new

Minecraft 1.13 (Update Aquatic) is a major Java Edition update released on July 18, 2018. Its biggest additions: New ocean biomes, coral reefs, shipwrecks, ocean ruins, buried treasure, icebergs; Dolphins, Turtles, Drowned, Phantoms and four kinds of fish; Trident.

What's new in 1.13

Bold lines are the headline changes.

Mobs

  • Dolphins, Turtles, Drowned, Phantoms and four kinds of fish

Blocks

  • Conduit, coral, kelp, sea pickles, blue ice, stripped logs

Items

  • Trident
  • Heart of the Sea, nautilus shells, turtle shell helmet

World & biomes

  • New ocean biomes, coral reefs, shipwrecks, ocean ruins, buried treasure, icebergs

Gameplay

  • Swimming and crawling

Technical

  • Data packs and a new command system

Heads-up

  • "The Flattening" changed every block and item ID. Old worlds convert automatically, but back them up first and expect old command blocks to break
