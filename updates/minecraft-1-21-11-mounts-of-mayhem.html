Minecraft 1.21.11 Mounts of Mayhem: what's new

Minecraft 1.21.11 (Mounts of Mayhem) is a major Java Edition update released on December 9, 2025. On Bedrock Edition the same update is version 1.21.130, released December 9, 2025. Its biggest additions: Spears in every tier: jab or charge, and damage grows with your speed; Nautilus: tame and ride it underwater; it gives you Breath of the Nautilus; Zombie Nautilus ridden by Drowned; Zombie Horses ridden by zombies with spears.

What's new in 1.21.11

Bold lines are the headline changes.

Mobs

  • Nautilus: tame and ride it underwater; it gives you Breath of the Nautilus
  • Zombie Nautilus ridden by Drowned; Zombie Horses ridden by zombies with spears
  • Camel Husk ridden by the Parched, a new desert skeleton

Items

  • Spears in every tier: jab or charge, and damage grows with your speed
  • Nautilus armor
  • Netherite horse armor

Gameplay

  • Lunge enchantment for spears

Technical

  • Texture filtering (anisotropic) option. Last version with the old 1.x numbering

Heads-up

  • Game rules were renamed. Check any commands or data packs that set them
